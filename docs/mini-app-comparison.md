# Mini App comparison worksheet

The goal is to compare the two ways of placing the conversation. The feature
screens and sample data stay the same. The demo does not collect analytics or
choose a preference from usage.

## Trial order

Run the four tasks once in Telegram companion mode, reset the demo, and repeat
in In-app assistant mode. Then reset and reverse the order on a phone-sized
viewport and on desktop.

| Task | What to try | What to notice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Correct a meal | Open the sample lunch, drag calories, enter an exact number, save | How easy the record is to find, precision, number of taps, confidence in the save |
| Log a workout | Enter three sets, correct one, finish a set, start a short rest timer | Whether the controls are easy to reach and edits feel safe |
| Change a reminder | Pick another date, inspect the weekday preview, snooze one occurrence | Whether the local time and recurring schedule are clear |
| Explore a result | Filter the chart and open a record | Whether conversation makes the controls easier or gets in the way |
